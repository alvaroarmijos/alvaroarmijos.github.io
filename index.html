About

Building software is a craft.

I'm a Flutter Engineer from Ecuador with over six years of experience turning product requirements into production-grade mobile applications. I've worked with teams across the Americas and Europe, shipping apps that users actually remember.

Currently at Very Good Ventures, I help clients bring their most ambitious Flutter products to life — covering architecture decisions, performance optimization, test coverage, and everything in between.
